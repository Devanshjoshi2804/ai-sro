# Receiving

The Receiving functionality provides visibility into incoming inventory and the ability to manage its receipt and allocation within the warehouse. The receiving process typically consists of planned or unplanned inventory being received from an inbound shipment, moved into an inbound order line, and then the distributions are allocated to specified warehouse locations.

For more information and setup tasks, see the [Receiving](configuration/inbound/receiving.md) configurations.
