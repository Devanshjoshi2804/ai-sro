# Workload Plan report

This report provides user availability and capacity to complete outstanding assignments based on the job code, including current assignments, estimated user availability, and the remaining user capacity for the shift. This report helps supervisors perform the following tasks:

-   Assign users to the correct jobs for optimal use of time
-   Plan for the completion of their workload by the end of their shift

After you select the report, you can provide a description of the report, specify the date range for which information is collected, and enter selection criteria to group or limit the output of the report.

**Note**: The Workload Plan report is accessible through Workforce Balance Operations.
