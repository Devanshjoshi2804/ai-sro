# Application overview

Warehouse Management is a warehouse and distribution center management application that combines advanced information technologies with industry leading functionality. It is designed to provide you with an effective real-time management solution, either as a standalone application or as a fully integrated component of the Supply Chain Execution (SCE) product line.

The following table lists the functional areas and capabilities of Warehouse Management.

 
| Functional Area | Capabilities |
| --- | --- |
| Warehouse configuration | General<br>-   • Simplified and intuitive design
<br>-   • Faster to implement and easier to maintain
<br > Workflows<br>-   • Let you manage unique or exception processes with no coding required
<br>-   • Subsequent processing is driven by user responses to questions or instructions
<br>-   • Workflows are triggered by various touch points, such as warehouse, supplier, item, carrier, customer, order type, and status |
| Third-Party logistics support | -   • Multi-client architecture
    -   • Controls inventory ownership
    <br>-   • Segregates data
    <br>-   • Limits user access and security
    <br>-   • Integrated in billing processes
    <br>
<br>-   • Client-specific or shared locations
<br>-   • Client configurable workflows
<br>-   • Clients can span multiple facilities
<br>-   • Users can be directed to work only on certain client tasks
<br>-   • Reports, labels, and alerts can be segregated by client
<br>-   • Advanced third-party billing capabilities |
| Inbound operations | Yard management<br>-   • Full yard management capabilities for tracking the activities and contents of the receiving, shipping, and storage transport equipment in the yard
<br>-   • Yard audits for verifying the transport equipment at a yard location
<br>-   • Extended inventory visibility and operational capabilities
<br>-   • Labor estimates (if integrated with Warehouse Labor Management) drive expected duration for appointment and optimal dock door
<br > Receiving<br>-   • Inbound advanced shipment notification of specific inventory
<br>-   • Identify and track total quantity of an order on one or more inbound shipments
<br>-   • Cross docking capabilities
<br>-   • Monitor inbound quality
<br > Putaway<br>-   • System-suggested storage location optimizes product placement based on item and location characteristics, location capacity, inventory mixing restrictions, and other attributes
<br>-   • Movement paths with pickup-and-deposit locations facilitate material flow and enforce equipment restrictions
<br>-   • Ability to override a storage location is configurable
<br>-   • Storage suggestion logic first considers possible cross docks (such as to outbound or production) to minimize product touches
<br>-   • Configurable rules determine optimal storage location, considering item, item family, supplier, status, receiving point, and other attributes
<br > Returns<br>-   • Returns are linked to outbound order to enhance return processing
<br>-   • Includes unexpected inventory in-line with standard returns processing
<br>-   • Rule-based disposition is based on customer return reason and item
<br>-   • Supports next-action processing, such as replacement and return to stock |
| Inventory management | General<br>-   • Extensive capabilities for lot control, serialization, and date processing
<br>-   • Multi-level inventory holds with configurable hold characteristics, controlled locally or across the network
<br>-   • Detailed transaction audit provides inventory processing traceability
<br>-   • Real-time, system-directed summary and audit cycle counts with configurable cost and quantity thresholds
<br>-   • Scheduled ABC counts supplemented by opportunistic and exception counts
<br > Product placement<br>-   • System-directed storage location based on user-defined rules directs product to the most optimal location
<br>-   • Automatic velocity recalculation can adjust item characteristics and re-direct new product to a different velocity zone
<br>-   • Re-slotting allows for product repositioning at predetermined intervals to accommodate seasonal adjustments, product promotions, and the like
<br > Work orders<br>-   • Work order management capabilities for kitting, assembly, and production
    -   • Assembly and disassembly
    <br>-   • Bill of material administration
    <br>-   • Component allocation, picking, and line delivery
    <br>-   • Component-level tracking by attributes such as lot, date, and serial number
    <br>-   • Work order reconciliation (scrap, return to storage, consumption)
    <br> |
| Outbound operations | Wave management<br>-   • Configurable wave rules such as by store and departure time pull orders into waves
<br > Pick and replenish<br>-   • RF, voice, paper or label picking: any or all types in a single environment
<br>-   • Multi-method picking such as order, bulk, and zone, in a single environment
<br>-   • User-defined pick verification such as by location or lot
<br>-   • Cartonization
    -   • Calculate appropriate container based on products to be packaged while considering nesting factors and product mixing restrictions
    <br>-   • Supports picking, shipping, and pack station cartonization
    <br>-   • Supports automatic (application determines carton) and manual (operator determines carton) processing
    <br>
<br>-   • Replenish locations on demand, by minimum-maximum levels, and by top-off: any or all types in a single environment
<br > Pack station<br>-   • Touch-screen enabled pack station functionality
<br>-   • Cartonization and pack station order consolidation
<br>-   • Scan verification to capture UPC, lot, serial number, and other attributes
<br>-   • In-line parcel execution
<br > Loading<br>-   • Fluid or live loading, stop sequence, and multiple zones provide control in directed and undirected modes
<br > Documents<br>-   • User-configurable labels and documents for customer and carrier compliance
<br > Outbound ASNs<br>-   • Outbound advance shipment notification (ASN) generation providing downstream efficiency |
| Task management | -   • Determines the next most optimal task for one operator as opposed to another operator
<br>-   • Tasks assignments evaluated against permission, priority, and proximity
<br>-   • Tasks have configurable base priorities and escalation profiles
<br>-   • All available tasks are managed in a single work queue
<br>-   • User and equipment authorizations |
| User experience | -   • Mobility solution provides supervisors and managers desk-free operability
<br>-   • Web-based application provides real-time visibility and actionable information
<br>-   • Flexibly respond to business demands in and outside the facility
<br>-   • Troubleshoot and solve problems before they impact the operation |

## Integrations

Warehouse Management supports integration with the following applications:

-   Connect (Platform Data Cloud)
-   Data Access Service
    -   Snowflake
-   Event Management
-   Integrator (Supply Chain Execution)
-   Luminate Billing
-   Third-party parcel applications (integrated through Parcel Handler)
    -   Centiro
    -   ProShip
    -   Sendflex
-   Transportation Manager
-   Warehouse Execution System
    -   Robotics Hub
    -   Warehouse Tasking
-   Warehouse Labor Management
