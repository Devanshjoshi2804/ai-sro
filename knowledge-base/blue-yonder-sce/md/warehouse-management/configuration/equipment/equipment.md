# Equipment

Equipment refers to the various types of equipment (such as forklifts, trucks, and trailers) used in warehouse and transport operations. Every type of equipment that you want to use with the application must be configured and defined.

When you configure equipment, you define its equipment type and the location access groups to which that equipment belongs.
