# Standard reports for Modeling functionality

The Modeling functionality in Warehouse Labor Management provides you with a standard set of reports that you can run to view the status of activities within your facility. For example, you can run a Discrete Flowchart report to view all or a range of discrete flowcharts for a company or warehouse.
