# Internationalization

Internationalization enables the application to operate in different locales; that is, in different languages and regions of the world. Cultural features, such as address and telephone number formats, can be configured or customized to operate according to the locale preference of the application or user. You use Internalization pages to configure details for countries, states, currencies, city postal codes, measurement units, message catalogs, time zone displays, and locales.
