# Distribution Status Display

You can view the current condition of distributions in the warehouse, including merge-in-transit distributions. Specifically, you can view the information for the inventory associated with a distribution.

## View distribution status

1.  Select **Inventory > Distribution Status Display**.
    
2.  In the top grid, select a distribution record.
    
3.  Under **DISTRIBUTION STATUS INVENTORY**, view the status information for the selected distribution.
