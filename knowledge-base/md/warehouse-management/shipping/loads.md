# Loads

The Loads page provides visibility to the existing outbound loads and the picking and loading status of the loads. A load is a collection of shipments that are shipped together on the same piece of transport equipment. For more information, see the [Loads](shipping-concepts.md) shipping concept.

You can use the Loads page to manage the shipments and orders on a load, the transport equipment associated with a load, and the load itself. You can also view additional load details and perform tasks by accessing the following information from the Loads dashboard:

-   **Loads page**: The main page shows the loads that have been created and high level information for each load such as the number of stops, transport equipment details, and the percentage of inventory that has been picked and loaded.
    
    The grid is sorted in ascending order (earliest to latest) by the **Departure** column (appointment end date and time) so that the load scheduled to depart first is displayed at the top.
    
-   **Load details**: When you click on a load identifier on the Loads page, additional load details are displayed. You can edit load details, load a stop, assign shipments to the load, and perform other tasks relevant to the inventory, equipment, and stops associated with the load. Additional stop information includes the customer details and picking and loading progress for each specific stop.
-   **Stop details**: When you click on a stop identifier while viewing load details, the stop details are displayed. You can view the picking and loading progress and other detailed information for each shipment assigned to the stop, and you can perform tasks relevant to the shipments on a stop including assigning a new shipment to a stop.
-   **Shipment and order details**: When you click a shipment while viewing the stop details, the inventory details for the shipment are displayed. Similarly, if you expand a shipment on a stop and click an order instead, the inventory details specific to that order are displayed. You can perform tasks such as unpicking inventory or moving inventory to a different shipment.
-   **LPN details**: When you click an LPN while viewing shipment or order details, inventory details specific to that single LPN are displayed. You can view basic LPN details, additional inventory attributes, date-controlled inventory information, shipping details, and if applicable, serialization information. You can also perform tasks such as unpicking inventory or moving inventory to a different shipment.
