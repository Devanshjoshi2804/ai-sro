Transport Equipment

In this topic
  • Transport Equipment
    • Yard and door audits

The Transport Equipment page is accessible from the following modules: Receiving, Shipping, or Yard.

This page provides visibility to all the existing, expected, or dispatched transport equipment and tractors in your facility. Within the application, pieces of transport equipment are considered to be vehicles that transport product to and from a warehouse. Trailers, rail cars, sea containers, and other types of containers used for the transportation or storage of goods are all referred to as transport equipment. Vehicles used to haul the transport equipment, such as tractors, rail engines, and ships, are referred to as tractors.

The Transport Equipment page consists of the following tabs:

  • Transport Equipment: Displays details of the transport equipment. You can use this tab to perform the following tasks:
    • Create shipping, receiving, and storage transport equipment
    • Check in transport equipment and assigned tractor to a yard or dock door location, and perform workflows for transport equipment if prompted to do so
    • Move transport equipment and assigned tractor to a yard or dock door location, and perform workflows for transport equipment if prompted to do so
    • View workflow history
    • Modify the carrier or identifier for transport equipment
    • Associate tractors to transport equipment
    • Generate a location audit
  • Tractors: Displays details of the tractors. You can use this tab to perform the following tasks:
    • View tractor information
    • Create or modify tractors and associate them with transport equipment
    • Check in tractors (with or without transport equipment) to a yard or dock door location
    • Check out tractors from a yard or dock door location
    • Modify the carrier or identifier for tractor

Yard and door audits

You can request a yard or dock door (location) audit to verify that your yard information is accurate. For example, you can determine whether a yard or door location is empty or full, or whether specified transport equipment is actually parked in a location.

Note: You cannot use location audits to audit the inventory that is on transport equipment. Additionally, you cannot use audits to confirm whether the tractors assigned to transport equipment are actually parked in a location.

Audits are requested at a workstation and sent to the work queue to be distributed to and performed by RF operators in the yard. When RF operators perform an audit, they are required to confirm transport equipment in the audit locations, or verify that a location is empty. If expected equipment is not found in an audit location, the application applies the Missing Equipment tag to the location.

If a location fails an audit, you can reconcile the audit by updating the equipment's actual location in the application.

You can view scheduled audits on the Work Queue page, and you can view audit results on the History page (Inventory tab).
