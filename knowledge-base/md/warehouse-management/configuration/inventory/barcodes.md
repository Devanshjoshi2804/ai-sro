# Barcodes

A barcode can be scanned to extract and display information (such as item, lot, and manufactured date) to users.

When you configure barcodes, you define how the application stores and processes data parsed from a GS1 barcode. You can also create templates to simplify warehouse processes and provide reliable data entry.
