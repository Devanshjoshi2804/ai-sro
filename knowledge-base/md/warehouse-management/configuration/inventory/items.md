# Items

An item is any specific product that is stored or processed by the application. Items can be raw materials, packaging materials, in-process materials, supplies, and finished goods. In some warehouses, items are referred to as stock keeping units (SKUs).

Before the application can effectively store, track, and manipulate inventory, every item within your facility must be assigned a unique item identifier, and its attributes must be defined. When defining an item, you specify information such as how a product is packaged and handled, along with details that the application can use to determine how a product should be received, stored, allocated, shipped, counted, and valued (cost information).

Although some host applications send item information to Warehouse Management, it is not unusual for a user, such as a receiving supervisor, to manually define a new item in the application or modify the attributes of an item before it is received for the first time.
