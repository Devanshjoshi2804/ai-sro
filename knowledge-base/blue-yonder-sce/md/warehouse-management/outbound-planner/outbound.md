# Outbound

The Outbound page provides visibility to existing orders, shipments, and loads. Additionally, you can create new orders, shipments, and loads.

When you first access the Outbound page, if you want to manage or view information for existing orders, shipments, or loads, you must enter search criteria to retrieve the data. Alternatively, you can filter the information that is displayed by selecting a quick filter.

The Outbound page consists of the following tabs:

**Note**: The number of orders, shipments, or loads that match the criteria is displayed on the tabs.

-   **Orders**: Access additional information for the orders that match the search criteria. Additionally, use this tab to add or modify an order or order line, or cancel or delete an order, among other tasks.
-   **Shipments**: Access additional information for the shipments that match the search criteria. Additionally, use this tab to add or modify a shipment, cancel or delete a shipment, or assign a shipment to a staging lane or load, among other tasks.
-   **Loads**: Access additional information for the loads that match the search criteria. Additionally, use this tab to add or modify a load, delete a load, or assign a load to a staging lane, among other tasks.

When you click an identifier on the Outbound page, additional details are displayed as well as tabs that provide visibility to the following information related to the order, shipment, or load:

**Note**: Depending on the tab you select, you can also perform additional tasks related to the information displayed on the tab.

-   Order lines in an order
-   Order activity
-   Orders in a shipment
-   Stops, orders, and shipments in a load
-   Picks
-   LPNs
-   Short inventory
-   Pending replenishments
-   Customs information, if enabled
-   Cross dock opportunities
