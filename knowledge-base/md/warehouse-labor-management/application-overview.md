# Application overview

Warehouse Labor Management is an extensive labor scheduling and reporting solution designed to help management personnel maximize employee performance and facility operation through improved planning, workload balancing, feedback, and productivity reporting. It provides you with an effective, real-time management solution, either as a standalone application or as a fully integrated component of the Supply Chain Execution (SCE) product line.

The following table lists the functional areas and capabilities of Warehouse Labor Management.

 
| Functional Area | Capabilities |
| --- | --- |
| Planning | General<br>-   •
    
    Streamline labor data administration and reporting
    
    <br>
<br>-   •
    
    Planning profile configurations for display of progression statistics and planning information
    
    <br>
<br>-   •
    
    Labor and workforce planning capabilities
    
    <br>
<br > Modeling<br>-   •
    
    Integrates engineering standards data (such as patterns, timings, and master standard data \[MSD\] elements) into the application and provides the ability to edit and manage that data for your environment.
    
    <br> |

    Monitoring based on discrete (detailed) standards and quality measures

    <br>
<br>-   •

    <br>
