# Policy

A policy is a rule that you can define to direct or change the way in which your application functions. You can use policies to customize your application to meet your unique operational needs.

Policies can be configured using the following pages:

-   **Policy Maintenance**: Lists individual policies for any area of functionality. See [Policy Maintenance](policy/policy-maintenance.md).
-   **Policy Maintenance - Override**: Lists polices that have been added or can be overridden for a specific warehouse in a multi-warehouse environment. See [Policy Maintenance - Override](policy/policy-maintenance-override.md).
-   **Policy History**: Lists historical information about the policies that have been added, changed, and deleted. See [Policy History](policy/policy-history.md).

**Note**: Many of the fields on **Warehouse Management > Configuration** pages affect policy values. Therefore, when you update a configuration page field, the update is applied to its related policy value; and when you update a policy value, the update is applied to its related configuration page field.

The various policies that these pages manage are also described in this section.
