# ADR NNN — <decision, as a statement>

**Status:** proposed | accepted | superseded by ADR NNN · <release>

## Context

What forced the decision. The constraint, the failure mode, or the question that
could not be deferred. Written so a reader who was not there understands why this
was not obvious.

## Decision

What was chosen, stated plainly.

Include what was **rejected** and why — that is the part nobody can reconstruct
later, and the part that stops the same debate reopening.

## Consequences

What this buys, and what it costs. Include the costs honestly; an ADR with no
downsides was not a real decision.
