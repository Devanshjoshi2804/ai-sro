# Customers

A customer is a business that receives shipped inventory. Each customer has a profile that determines how their inventory is handled, processed, and shipped.

You use the Customers pages to define and maintain profiles for every customer for whom you plan to ship inventory. Additionally, you define customer types to group customers that have the same order processing requirements.
