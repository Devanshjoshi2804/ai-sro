# Reports

The application provides a standard set of reports that you can run to view the status of activities within your facility.

When working with reports, you can perform the following tasks:

-   Generate and view a PDF version of a report.
-   Change the appearance of a report on your computer display, for example, to zoom in or out.
-   Print a report.
-   Export report data to your computer in several different formats: CSV, HTML, Microsoft Excel (XLS), and PDF.

The reports are generated and displayed on the **System Administrator > Reports/labels > Operations** page. See [Operations](../administration/system-administrator/reports-labels/operations.md).

The reports are configured on the **System Administrator > Reports/labels > Reports** page. See [Reports - Administration](../administration/system-administrator/reports-labels/reports.md).

**IMPORTANT**: To view and generate reports, Warehouse Reporting must be installed and integrated with the Blue Yonder application.
