Application overview

In this topic
  • Application overview
    • Integrations

Warehouse Management is a warehouse and distribution center management application that combines advanced information technologies with industry leading functionality. It is designed to provide you with an effective real-time management solution, either as a standalone application or as a fully integrated component of the Supply Chain Execution (SCE) product line.

The following table lists the functional areas and capabilities of Warehouse Management.

Functional Area

Capabilities

Warehouse configuration

General

  • Simplified and intuitive design
  • Faster to implement and easier to maintain

Workflows

  • Let you manage unique or exception processes with no coding required
  • Subsequent processing is driven by user responses to questions or instructions
  • Workflows are triggered by various touch points, such as warehouse, supplier, item, carrier, customer, order type, and status

Third-Party logistics support
  • Multi-client architecture
    • Controls inventory ownership
    • Segregates data
    • Limits user access and security
    • Integrated in billing processes
  • Client-specific or shared locations
  • Client configurable workflows
  • Clients can span multiple facilities
  • Users can be directed to work only on certain client tasks
  • Reports, labels, and alerts can be segregated by client
  • Advanced third-party billing capabilities

Inbound operations

Yard management

  • Full yard management capabilities for tracking the activities and contents of the receiving, shipping, and storage transport equipment in the yard
  • Yard audits for verifying the transport equipment at a yard location
  • Extended inventory visibility and operational capabilities
  • Labor estimates (if integrated with Warehouse Labor Management) drive expected duration for appointment and optimal dock door

Receiving

  • Inbound advanced shipment notification of specific inventory
  • Identify and track total quantity of an order on one or more inbound shipments
  • Cross docking capabilities
  • Monitor inbound quality

Putaway

  • System-suggested storage location optimizes product placement based on item and location characteristics, location capacity, inventory mixing restrictions, and other attributes
  • Movement paths with pickup-and-deposit locations facilitate material flow and enforce equipment restrictions
  • Ability to override a storage location is configurable
  • Storage suggestion logic first considers possible cross docks (such as to outbound or production) to minimize product touches
  • Configurable rules determine optimal storage location, considering item, item family, supplier, status, receiving point, and other attributes

Returns

  • Returns are linked to outbound order to enhance return processing
  • Includes unexpected inventory in-line with standard returns processing
  • Rule-based disposition is based on customer return reason and item
  • Supports next-action processing, such as replacement and return to stock

Inventory management

General

  • Extensive capabilities for lot control, serialization, and date processing
  • Multi-level inventory holds with configurable hold characteristics, controlled locally or across the network
  • Detailed transaction audit provides inventory processing traceability
  • Real-time, system-directed summary and audit cycle counts with configurable cost and quantity thresholds
  • Scheduled ABC counts supplemented by opportunistic and exception counts

Product placement

  • System-directed storage location based on user-defined rules directs product to the most optimal location
  • Automatic velocity recalculation can adjust item characteristics and re-direct new product to a different velocity zone
  • Re-slotting allows for product repositioning at predetermined intervals to accommodate seasonal adjustments, product promotions, and the like

Work orders

  • Work order management capabilities for kitting, assembly, and production
    • Assembly and disassembly
    • Bill of material administration
    • Component allocation, picking, and line delivery
    • Component-level tracking by attributes such as lot, date, and serial number
    • Work order reconciliation (scrap, return to storage, consumption)

Outbound operations

Wave management

  • Configurable wave rules such as by store and departure time pull orders into waves

Pick and replenish

  • RF, voice, paper or label picking: any or all types in a single environment
  • Multi-method picking such as order, bulk, and zone, in a single environment
  • User-defined pick verification such as by location or lot
  • Cartonization
    • Calculate appropriate container based on products to be packaged while considering nesting factors and product mixing restrictions
    • Supports picking, shipping, and pack station cartonization
    • Supports automatic (application determines carton) and manual (operator determines carton) processing
  • Replenish locations on demand, by minimum-maximum levels, and by top-off: any or all types in a single environment

Pack station

  • Touch-screen enabled pack station functionality
  • Cartonization and pack station order consolidation
  • Scan verification to capture UPC, lot, serial number, and other attributes
  • In-line parcel execution

Loading

  • Fluid or live loading, stop sequence, and multiple zones provide control in directed and undirected modes

Documents

  • User-configurable labels and documents for customer and carrier compliance

Outbound ASNs

  • Outbound advance shipment notification (ASN) generation providing downstream efficiency

Task management
  • Determines the next most optimal task for one operator as opposed to another operator
  • Tasks assignments evaluated against permission, priority, and proximity
  • Tasks have configurable base priorities and escalation profiles
  • All available tasks are managed in a single work queue
  • User and equipment authorizations

User experience
  • Mobility solution provides supervisors and managers desk-free operability
  • Web-based application provides real-time visibility and actionable information
  • Flexibly respond to business demands in and outside the facility
  • Troubleshoot and solve problems before they impact the operation

Integrations

Warehouse Management supports integration with the following applications:

  • Connect (Platform Data Cloud)
  • Data Access Service
    • Snowflake
  • Event Management
  • Integrator (Supply Chain Execution)
  • Luminate Billing
  • Third-party parcel applications (integrated through Parcel Handler)
    • Centiro
    • ProShip
    • Sendflex
  • Transportation Manager
  • Warehouse Execution System
    • Robotics Hub
    • Warehouse Tasking
  • Warehouse Labor Management
