# Staging

The Staging page is accessible from the following modules: **Receiving** or **Shipping**.

Staging provides visibility to the utilization of staging lanes and dock doors. You can also monitor the receiving and storage progress of an inbound shipment or the picking and loading progress of an outbound load. Additionally, you can perform multiple tasks related to the inbound or outbound inventory and transport equipment.

The Staging dashboard is divided into the following two areas:

-   **Lanes**: Use the Lanes area to view and manage the current activity for staging lanes. Current activity means that inventory is being received to a lane for storage or being staged in a lane to be loaded for shipping. Additionally, you can view pending inventory moves from one lane to another, and manage staged shipments that are not assigned to an outbound load.
-   **Doors**: Use the Doors area to view and manage the transport equipment that is checked in to a dock door. You can also view the receiving and shipping status for the inbound shipment or outbound load associated to each piece of equipment. Additionally, you can view pending equipment moves from one door (or yard location) to another, and manage the details and work associated to the equipment.
