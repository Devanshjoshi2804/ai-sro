# Parcel

The Parcel page provides visibility to the outbound parcel shipments, packages, and manifests in the warehouse. Parcel processing is the act of preparing and shipping orders using a small package (parcel) carrier. Most parcel carriers handle individual, smaller packages (typically weighing less than 150 pounds), but some parcel carriers offer heavy parcel services as well.

**IMPORTANT**: To perform parcel processing, Warehouse Management must be integrated with a parcel application through Parcel Handler. See the _Warehouse Management Parcel Handler Configuration Guide_.

The Parcel page includes the following tabs:

-   **Shipments**: A parcel shipment is a shipment of one or more orders that is shipped using a small package carrier. The parcel is the shippable entity, while the parcel shipment is a grouping of parcels that is shipped together to the same destination. You can view information for parcel shipments that have not yet been shipped.
-   **Packages**: A parcel package is a small package that contains inventory and is to be shipped, using a parcel carrier, to a customer. In Warehouse Management, a parcel can be a sub-LPN, case, kit, carton, or non-Warehouse Management package (such as a business office parcel or an employee's personal parcel). You can view parcel packages, as well as release or void a package, and print a parcel label.
-   **Manifests**: A manifest is a group of parcel packages all being shipped by the same carrier at the same time, and to which tracking numbers and rates are assigned. A manifest list identifies all parcels that are shipped together on the same piece of transport equipment by the parcel carrier. A manifest can be open or closed.
    -   **Open**: When parcels are being manifested and added to the carrier's manifest list, the manifests are considered open. Parcels can be added or removed from a manifest, but a manifest must be closed in order to be shipped. You can view and close open manifests.
    -   **Closed**: When the parcel carrier's transport equipment is ready to depart and all of the manifested parcels are loaded onto the truck, the manifest is closed. Once a manifest is closed, it cannot be reopened. You can view closed manifests, print paperwork for a closed manifest, or complete a shipment in a closed manifest.
