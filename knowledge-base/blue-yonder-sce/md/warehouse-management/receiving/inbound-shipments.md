# Inbound Shipments - Receiving

You use the Inbound Shipments page to view and manage inbound shipments and orders, and the LPNs that are received against them. Inbound shipment and order tracking is the process of monitoring the progress of existing inbound orders, regardless of their status, against the expected receiving activity.

You can view additional shipment details and perform tasks by accessing the following information from the Inbound Shipments page:

-   **Inbound Shipments page**: The main page shows the inbound shipments that have been created and high level information for each inbound shipment such as receiving status, number of orders, and the percentage of inventory that has been received and stored.
-   **Shipment details**: When you click on a shipment identifier on the Inbound Shipments page, additional shipment details are displayed. You can view information for each order or the LPNs on the shipment, and you can add a new or existing order to the shipment.
-   **Order details**: When you click on a planned inbound order identifier while viewing shipment details, the detailed order information is displayed. You can view information for each order line on the order, and you can add or remove lines.
-   **Order line details**: When you click on an order line while viewing order details, the line details are displayed. You can select each line to view or edit the order line information.
-   **LPN details**: After receiving has started for a shipment or order, you can access detailed LPN information for the received inventory from the shipment details page. You can view inventory attribute details and information related to date-tracked inventory, and you can also edit or reverse receive an LPN. You can also view LPN details for an inbound shipment that has not been received if it is associated with an advanced shipment notification (ASN).
