# Bill of Lading

This report displays an industry standard Bill of Lading, as defined by Voluntary Interindustry Commerce Standards (VICS). The Bill of Lading is a carrier's contract and receipt for goods that it agrees to transport from one place to another, and to deliver to a designated person or assignees.

**Note**: Customer-specific bills of lading may also be available.
