# Shipping

The Shipping functionality provides visibility into outgoing inventory and the ability to manage its transport from the warehouse with all the required paperwork and transactions. The shipping process typically consists of staging the shipment, assigning it to an outbound load, printing paperwork, loading transport equipment, and then closing and dispatching the transport equipment.

For more information and setup tasks, see the [Shipping](configuration/outbound/shipping.md) configurations.
