# Production

The Production functionality provides visibility into production lines and work orders, as well as the ability to manage production processes by planning and scheduling work order assignments. The production process typically consists of work orders dictating the sequence of what items are taken and combined to produce new items. It can also consist of item disassembly and manipulation for the creation of different items.

For more information and setup tasks, see the [Production](configuration/production.md) configurations.
