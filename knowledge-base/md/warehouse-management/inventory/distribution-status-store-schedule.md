# Distribution Status Store Schedule

You can view the current condition of distributions in the warehouse, including merge-in-transit distributions. Specifically, you can view and the days of the week and times during which inventory to be distributed to the store is to be loaded onto outbound transport equipment.

## View distribution status store schedule

1.  Select **Inventory > Distribution Status Store Schedule**.
    
2.  In the top grid, select a distribution record.
    
3.  Under **STORE SCHEDULE**, view schedule information for the selected distribution.
