# Door Activity

The Door Activity page is accessible from the following modules: **Receiving**, **Shipping**, or **Yard**.

Door activity provides visibility to the utilization of dock doors and yard locations. You can monitor the receiving and shipping progress as it relates to the transport equipment checked in to a dock door, or you can view the equipment parked in a yard location.

This page is divided into the following two areas:

-   **Doors**: Use the Doors area to view and manage the transport equipment that is checked in to a dock door. You can also view the receiving and shipping status for the inbound shipment or outbound load associated to each piece of equipment. Additionally, you can view and manage the current and upcoming appointments for each dock door.
-   **Yard Locations**: Use the Yard Locations area to view and manage the transport equipment in your yard locations. This can include both receiving and shipping equipment, as well as storage equipment.
