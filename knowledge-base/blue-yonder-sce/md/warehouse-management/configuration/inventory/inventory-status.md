# Inventory Status

An inventory status is used to define the physical condition, availability, or material handling requirements of inventory.

You use the Inventory Status pages to configure the statuses available to define the state of inventory with, as well as what user roles can perform status changes and what statuses, by pick zone, cannot be picked. You can also define a series of inventory statuses associated with product or acceptable quality levels.
