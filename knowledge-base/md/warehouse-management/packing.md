# Packing

The Packing functionality provides visibility into pack locations with pending work and the ability to manage the options available to operators when packing containers. The packing process consists of moving inventory out of a picking container and into one or more shipping containers, then moving that container to a staging location. The application accounts for errors and exceptions that may occur during the packing process, such as damaged or unexpected inventory.

For more information and setup tasks, see the [Packing](configuration/outbound/packing.md) configurations.
