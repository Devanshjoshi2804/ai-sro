# Carriers

A carrier is an individual or company that transports product from a designated origin to a designated destination. The application uses carrier information during receiving, order processing, and shipping operations.

When you configure carriers, you define active carrier records, how freight is transported from one location to another, the generation of progressive rotating order (PRO) numbers (which the application automatically assigns to shipments), and the carrier and service level associations between Warehouse Management and an external system.
